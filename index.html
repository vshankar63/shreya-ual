02

How I see this role

North star

Funding support that academics barely notice, because it just works.

The challenge

UAL’s research and knowledge exchange is ambitious, often international, and often multi-partner. The funders behind it — from Horizon Europe and UKRI to Innovate UK and EuropeAid — each have their own rules, systems and audit expectations. The project manager’s job is to absorb that complexity so it never slows the research down.

Who this role serves

  • Academics: clear guidance before they apply, no surprises after award, and more time for research.
  • Funders and partners: accurate, timely reporting, audit-ready records, and one reliable point of contact.
  • UAL and RKEE: compliant projects, budgets that are regularly re-forecast, and more follow-on funding and impact from every award.

Likely barriers, and how I’d approach them

“Compliance feels like bureaucracy.”

I translate funder rules into short, practical guidance, explain why each rule exists, and only ask for what’s needed.

“Creative practice doesn’t always fit funder templates.”

I work with academics early on impact pathways and dissemination, so the application tells their story in the funder’s language.

These are hypotheses from the outside. I’d test them in my first 30 days.
